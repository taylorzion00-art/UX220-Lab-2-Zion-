Seraph Of The End: Backstory of Yuichiro

The best thing ive recently read is a manga series called Seraph Of The End. From what I can remember the story starts out in a post apocalypic world where a mysterious virus wipes out most of the adults in the world only leaving children behind as the survivers. In this story their are vampires that take advantage of the collapse of society and begin keeping the surviving children as sources of blood underground. The main charater, Yuichiro is forced to grow up in this environment with another main character named Mikaela aswell as the other children from the orphanage they belonged to,where all of the children here consider each other to be family. Eventually they make a plan to escape but their escape goes wrong when a vampire noble named Ferid comes and kills most of the children leaving only Yuichiro as the last survivor. Yuichiro manages to get pretty far in his escape and ends up reaching the surface world where he discovers that humans are still alive, apon finding this out he becomes detirmined to join the fight against the vampires.

Yuichiro's drive as a soldier

A few years later, Yuichiro becomes part of the Japanese Imperial Demon Army, where he learns to fight using weapons powered by demons. What stood out to me during this part of the story was how much Yuichiro's experiences affected the way he treated other people. At first, he was focused on revenge and getting stronger, he didnt want to depend on anyone else. As he spends more time with his classmates Shinoa, Yoichi, Kimizuki and Mitsuba they have all have became very close, almost like a second family to him. As the story progresses it becomes very complicated when Yuichiro discovers that Mika Actually survived, but instead of escaping like Yuichiro did Mika was turned into a vampire. This creates an interesting situation because Yuichiro and Mika still care deeply about each other, even though they are now on opposite sides.

What I really like about this story

As I continued reading this story, I realized that Seraph Of The End is not a simple clique of humans being good and vampires being bad. There are secretes within both sides, one of which being the experiments conducted on Yuichiro and other children from the Hyakuya Orphanage. The "Seraph Of The End" itself becomes an important mystery, and Yuichiro begins to learn that there is much more behind the destruction of the world than originally thought. To me, this one the most interesting part of the story because not only does this start the best arc of the series it also shifts from being about revenge on the vampires to a larger mystery about human experiments, demons, vampires, and the motives of the people in power. Even with everything happening around him, Yuichiro's main goal changes to be protecting Mika and the friends he now considers family.
