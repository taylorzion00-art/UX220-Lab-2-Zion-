Seraph Of The End: My favourite Book

The best thing ive recently read is a manga series called Seraph Of The End. From what I can remember the story starts out in a post apocalypic world where a mysterious virus wipes out most of the adults in the world only leaving children behind as the survivers. In this story their are vampires that take advantage of the collapse of society and begin keeping the surviving children as sources of blood underground. The main charater, Yuichiro is forced to grow up in this environment with another main character named Mikaela aswell as the other children from the orphanage they belonged to. All of the children here consider each other to be family and eventually make a plan to escape
